# PulseSense: two-minute demo

**0:00–0:15 — Problem.** “Remote check-ins leave people juggling wearable numbers, photos and notes. PulseSense brings those pieces together while showing exactly what leaves the app.” Open the dashboard.

**0:15–0:50 — Camera.** Show preparation steps and the 30-second scan. While it captures, explain face-region color changes and why weak signals are rejected. Use a live result only if accepted; otherwise show the retry feedback and move to a previously saved, clearly labeled experimental scan. Never substitute a synthetic number as a live result.

**0:50–1:15 — Visual tracking.** Open two consented photos of the same tracked area. Show the boundary, dates and notes. Explain why image-area percentages are not a clinical recovery score. Request or display a saved Gemini review with its processing disclosure.

**1:15–1:35 — Accessible summary.** Select readings for an appointment brief, show the sharing preview, and display the editable result. Play a short ElevenLabs narration. The core task is preparing a useful artifact, not chatting.

**1:35–1:50 — Analytics and transparency.** Show an actual Tiger Data sync and daily aggregates if configured. Open AI activity and Privacy center. Describe scan-to-cloud updates accurately as opt-in updates after saving, not continuous monitoring.

**1:50–2:00 — Outcome.** “One place to record, compare and prepare questions—with visible sources and sharing choices.” Show pulsesenseai.us only as live if deployment is complete. Explain that camera estimates remain experimental.

Before recording, complete one rehearsal with real integrations. Get consent for any personal photos or measurements shown publicly. Keep keys and account details out of the video. DigitalOcean is not part of this submission.
